Pricing is based on each creator profile's monthly earnings. Plans are auto-assigned — Starter ($49) for under $2,500/mo, Growth ($99) for $2,500–$10,000/mo, Pro ($199) for $10,000–$30,000/mo, and Agency ($349) for $30,000+/mo. Every plan includes every feature.
